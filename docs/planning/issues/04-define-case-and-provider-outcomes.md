# Define case work and provider outcomes

Type: grilling
Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Status: resolved
Assignee: operator with Codex
Parent: ../map.md
Blocked by: 03, 07

## Question

What work and outcomes must a case preserve across providers, agent sessions, and ambiguous replies? Define the relationship between messages, campaigns, cases, resources, submission attempts, tickets, and provider actions. Keep uncertain delivery, acknowledgement, recipient-specific blocking, provider-reported mitigation, independently evidenced removal, rejection, and unresolved requests distinguishable where they affect the next action.

Use the sampled AWS response that both reports mitigation and requests headers, the Postmark recipient-protection statement, and NiceNIC's refusal as scenarios. Decide what can honestly count toward progress and which follow-ups or escalations remain actionable.

## Comments

The operator accepted a seven-day default wait before preparing a single follow-up, then another seven-day wait before marking an unanswered destination stalled. Respect a provider's longer response interval. Material new evidence may justify another update. These checks happen on subsequent manual runs, and follow-ups require approval before sending.

The operator wants escalation preparation to include an ICANN Compliance case when appropriate, using the NiceNIC correspondence as a motivating example. Preserve the original report, evidence, provider replies, and chronology. Record a provider's actual statements and decisions without treating an inferred motive as an established fact. Research the applicable route before defining eligibility or assembling a complaint.

The operator accepted separate outcome categories for acknowledgement, recipient protection, sending-account suspension, resource removal, and mitigation reported with unknown scope. Preserve the provider's actual statement as evidence and distinguish it from independent observations. These categories will support future statistics without counting every response as a takedown.

The operator accepted one case for repeated phishing emails pointing to the same specific page, retaining both originals and separate reporting actions for each relevant provider. Repeated confirmations of the same removal count once. Sharing only a hosting provider does not combine cases.

## Answer

A case preserves related source messages and their assessments, resources, and destination-specific reporting actions. Repeated samples about the same specific phishing page can share a case across different sending services. Campaign links remain provisional evidence associations, not permission to merge unrelated cases or assume common ownership. A report may use an existing provider ticket when appropriate without erasing the separate resources or sending activities under investigation.

Track acknowledgement, recipient protection, sending-account suspension, resource removal, and mitigation with unknown scope separately. Preserve provider statements and independent observations as distinct evidence. Count repeated confirmations of the same removal once. Keep outstanding work separate from protective outcomes so a mitigation statement does not silently close an evidence request or another provider's unfinished action.

On a manual run after seven days without a substantive response, prepare one follow-up for approval. After another seven days without a response to the sent follow-up, mark that destination stalled. Longer provider-specified intervals take precedence for these workflow checks. These are product timings, not legal deadlines. Do not repeatedly remind a silent destination; material new evidence may justify an update.

Stalled work or a substantive refusal can prompt an escalation assessment. For an applicable registrar complaint, prepare a dossier with the original report, evidence, chronology, correspondence, and identified handling gaps. Follow the [researched ICANN requirements](../../../docs/research/registrar-escalation.md); the workflow timer alone does not establish a contractual breach. Preserve contradictions or reframing in the correspondence without presenting an inferred motive as fact. Escalation reports require approval, and required web forms remain for manual submission.
