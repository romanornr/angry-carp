# Decide ordinary-mail retention

Type: grilling
Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Status: resolved
Assignee: operator with planning agent
Parent: ../map.md
Blocked by: 11

## Question

After an acquired email is assessed as ordinary and no case is opened, should its complete original remain in Angry Carp's private store, or should only a minimal processing record remain? Distinguish temporary acquisition from long-term retention before choosing stored data structures.

## Context

Acquiring an ordinary email does not create a case. Complete originals for investigation remain important evidence. Earlier instructions to preserve originals must not silently settle retention for all ordinary mailbox traffic.

## Resolution

The operator rejected retaining ordinary emails that are not phishing or scams. Their content must not become a permanent Angry Carp archive merely because it was acquired for assessment. This decision does not approve a processing-record schema or a particular temporary-storage mechanism. Retain originals needed for investigations under the existing evidence policy.
