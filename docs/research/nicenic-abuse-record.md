# NiceNIC abuse record: public evidence

Checked 2026-09-21. This bounded review covers independent phishing and botnet measurements, ICANN's public notices, and published reporting offered as evidence of criminal recommendations. It did not visit criminal services, fetch suspected phishing sites, or submit reports.

Independent measurements establish substantial abuse associated with NiceNIC registrations. They support tracking repeat cases and preparing evidence for escalation. Complaint handling, actor recommendations, and organizational motives require separate evidence.

## Measured prevalence

Interisle's Cybercrime Information Center identifies **NICENIC INTERNATIONAL GROUP CO., LIMITED, IANA 3765** as follows:

| Observation period | Reported phishing domains | Published gTLD portfolio denominator | Rank by phishing domains |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| May 1, 2024–April 30, 2025 | 114,493 | 140,839 | 1 |
| May 1, 2025–April 30, 2026 | 272,696 | 237,724 | 1 |

The comparison reports phishing-domain growth of 138% and portfolio growth of 69%. These are the publisher's figures, not a fresh count of live domains. [CIC year-over-year registrar comparison](https://www.cybercrimeinfocenter.org/phishing-activity-year-over-year-registrars-may-april-2026).

For the later period, CIC also ranks NiceNIC first by its normalized score: **11,471.1 phishing domains per 10,000 domains under management**. Eligibility requires at least 30,000 managed domains and 25 phishing domains; registrar data cover gTLDs. CIC separately classifies **235,255** NiceNIC domains as malicious phishing registrations, also the highest count. The annual phishing count exceeds the published portfolio denominator. This is a period observation divided by a portfolio measure; it must not be described as “115% of current NiceNIC domains are phishing.” The checked table does not establish the denominator's exact snapshot date. These rankings measure observed abuse, not response times or intent. [CIC registrar tables and score definition](https://www.cybercrimeinfocenter.org/phishing-activity-in-registrars-may-april-2026).

Spamhaus independently recorded **614 newly registered botnet command-and-control domains** at NiceNIC during January–June 2025, ranking it third in that table. That was down 62% from 1,636 during July–December 2024. This table counts observed botnet registrations without a registrar portfolio denominator. Its different threat category and period should remain separate from CIC's phishing ranking. It does not establish the outcome of any particular complaint. [Spamhaus Botnet Threat Update, January–June 2025, pp. 15–16](https://content.spamhaus.org/22f3805e-27c4-4bd9-a06e-37df8dab3d8c.pdf).
